Three simple words. An emphasis on “we.” A declaration of who we are, and a question of who we might become. The future has never belonged to one person, one institution, or one generation. It has always been shaped by people willing to imagine something better — and courageous enough to build it.

This year, TEDxChicago brings together thinkers, artists, scientists, entrepreneurs, and changemakers united by a shared belief: that progress is possible, belonging matters, and the systems shaping our lives are not inevitable. They were built by people, and therefore can be redesigned by people. From Chicago, a city shaped by bold ideas, resilience, and reinvention, we invite the world to move from spectatorship to participation, from cynicism to possibility.

Because the future is not something we inherit.
It is something we create, together.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is TEDxChicago 2026 held?

At the Harris Theater for Music and Dance, 205 East Randolph St., Chicago, IL 60601 — in Millennium Park. Doors open at 10:00 AM on Saturday, October 3, 2026, with registration and coat check on Lobby Level 5.

How do I find my way around the venue?

The experience spans all four Harris Theater lobby levels: registration, coat check, and parking validation on Level 5; food, activations, and art on Levels 3 and 2 (plus the Vinyl Listening Lounge and balcony seating on 2); and orchestra seating, art, and food on Level 1. Questions on the day? Look for a TEDxCHI volunteer.

What is the schedule for the day?

Doors open at 10:00 AM. Session 1 (Begin Within) runs 11:00 AM–12:30 PM, followed by lunch; Session 2 (Between Us) runs 1:45–3:45 PM, followed by an intermission; Session 3 (What We Build) runs 5:00–6:30 PM, closing with the Leo High School Choir. Between sessions, explore food, art, and activations across all four lobby levels.

What is included with my ticket?

Every ticket is all-inclusive: hosted snacks, desserts, and catered appetizers; hosted drinks including beer, wine, coffee, and tea; talks from all 15 TEDxChicago speakers and 5 live performances; and the immersive pop-up galleries from 5 visual artists throughout the Harris Theater lobbies. All ticket tiers receive the full experience.

How do I get tickets?

Tickets are available online at our official ticketing page. Patrons purchasing on or after September 23 will be recognized during the on-stage program.

Will the talks be available online?

Yes — every TEDxChicago talk is professionally filmed and published to the TEDx Talks channel after the event. Follow TEDxChicago on social media for release announcements.

Where do I park, and is transit nearby?

The Harris Theater sits above the Millennium Park garages, and parking validation is available at registration on Lobby Level 5. The theater is also steps from CTA Loop stations and Metra’s Millennium Station.

Is the venue accessible?

Yes. The Harris Theater is wheelchair accessible, with a full-service elevator serving all lobby levels. For accommodation requests, contact the TEDxChicago team ahead of the event, or ask any TEDxCHI volunteer on the day.

Is anything happening after the main event?

Two things: the exclusive Chicago screening of Who’s the We? at 8:00 PM on October 3 at Pitch & Pour Supper Club (free; badge holders get priority entrance), and the TEDxChicago Salon: Redesigning the System with CannonDesign on October 8. Details in the Beyond the Stage section above.

How do I contact TEDxChicago?

Reach the team any time through the contact page. At the event, look for a TEDxCHI volunteer — they’ll point you in the right direction.
